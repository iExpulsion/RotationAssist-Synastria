# Rotation Assist: Synastria

Synastria's changes for [Rotation Assist](https://github.com/iExpulsion/RotationAssist), the one-button rotation
addon for WoW 3.3.5a. Rotation Assist only knows stock 3.3.5a; this module tells it what Synastria does
differently.

## Installing

1. Install [Rotation Assist](https://github.com/iExpulsion/RotationAssist/releases/latest) 1.4.0 or later first
   (1.0.0 works, without the global cooldown change below).
2. Download `RotationAssist_Synastria-<version>.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and extract
   it into `World of Warcraft\Interface\AddOns`, next to the `RotationAssist` folder.
3. Restart the game.

## What's Changed

- **Both Classes.** Rotations are made for, grouped by and matched to all of your character's classes, not just
  the first.
- **Class Pair Names.** A rotation for two classes shows Synastria's name for the pair, like Lich or Spellblade.
- **Spec Names.** The spec dropdown and the minimap tooltip show your specs' names.
- **Mark of Frost.** Smart Picks waits for its Frost rune, like the stock Death Knight spells.
- **No Disconnects From Perk Spells.** Hold Presses During the Global Cooldown starts off. With it on, casting a
  spell a perk changes (like Curse of Agony with Bane) from a Smart Picks rotation disconnects you. If you turn it
  back on in Settings, leave such spells out of your rotations.
